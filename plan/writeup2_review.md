## What each agent did right

### DeepSeek — the better foundation

Selected as base

Its strongest contribution is the logical progression: acknowledge the failed grand synthesis, narrow the scope, explain the LTB model, separate established background from the open question, and identify the missing derivation. ### Qwen — the better source of narrative energy

Qwen’s most valuable contribution is its accessibility. The “forensic accountant,” “bumpy road,” and “blindfolded passenger” imagery gives general readers an intuitive entry into inverse problems and cosmological backreaction. Those claims are not established by the materials we reviewed. Nor does a model-fitting degeneracy alone show that backreaction differs between the models.

## The most important scientific corrections

The revised document makes four changes that matter more than stylistic polishing.

1\. Model non-uniqueness is not the same as backreaction non-identifiability. Different LTB models might reproduce the same distance–redshift relation. But they could still agree on the particular value of Buchert backreaction we want to determine. Our central result must address that quantity specifically.

2\. The Sussman result is stated conditionally. The sign of backreaction is subject to restrictions that depend on the LTB model class and averaging domain. His work includes vanishing-backreaction cases and more specific sign results; it does not justify a universal “closed means negative, open means positive” rule.&#x20;

4\. The research question now matches our current objective.
